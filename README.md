### 111\. What is an email campaign?

**Answer:**

> An email campaign is a planned set of emails sent to a target audience for a specific purpose, such as promotion, communication, engagement or generating leads.

### 112\. What is an Email Service Provider?

**Answer:**

> An Email Service Provider, or ESP, is a platform or service that helps organizations create, manage and send email campaigns. It may provide features such as list management, segmentation, automation and campaign analytics.

### 113\. What is an MTA?

**Answer:**

> MTA stands for Mail Transfer Agent. It is software responsible for transferring email messages between mail servers, commonly using SMTP.

### 114\. What is SMTP?

**Answer:**

> SMTP stands for Simple Mail Transfer Protocol. It is a protocol used to send and transfer email messages between mail systems.

### 115\. Difference between ESP and MTA?

**Answer:**

> An ESP is a broader email service platform that may provide campaign management, subscriber management, analytics and other features. An MTA is the mail-transfer software responsible for moving email messages between mail systems.

### 116\. What happens when an email is sent?

**Answer:**

> The email client submits the message to a mail server. The sending mail system determines the appropriate destination mail server and transfers the message, generally using SMTP. The receiving server accepts the message and places it in the recipient's mailbox, subject to its filtering and delivery policies.

### 117\. What is email segmentation?

**Answer:**

> Email segmentation means dividing an email audience into smaller groups based on characteristics such as interests, behavior, location or engagement so that communication can be more relevant.

### 118\. What is email list management?

**Answer:**

> Email list management involves maintaining subscriber data, managing subscriptions and unsubscribes, handling bounced addresses, organizing segments and keeping the list clean.

### 119\. What is email personalization?

**Answer:**

> Personalization means tailoring email content to individual recipients using relevant information, such as their name, preferences or previous interactions.

### 120\. What is email deliverability?
